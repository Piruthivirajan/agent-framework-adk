Quarterly platform review, Q3.

Migration. The order-processing service moved from a self-managed message broker to Amazon SQS in March. The cut-over caused 12 minutes of delayed processing, no lost messages. Dead-letter queues were added for every consumer, with an alarm when more than 50 messages are waiting.

Cost. Monthly compute spend fell by 12 percent after the move to Graviton instances and the removal of three idle staging clusters. Storage spend rose 4 percent because of longer log retention that audit asked for.

Reliability. Two incidents were recorded. The first was a certificate expiry on the internal API gateway, caught by customers before monitoring. The second was a runaway batch job that exhausted database connections for 25 minutes.

Next quarter. Automate certificate renewal, add connection pooling limits per job, and run a game day for the regional failover plan.
